Lab 1 — Propulsion (Dynamometer)

Students wire an ESC, motor, and propeller onto an instrumented test stand and measure thrust, torque, RPM, current, and voltage as a function of PWM command. Measurements are then fit to the propulsion models developed in lecture to extract thrust and torque coefficients, Kv, and efficiency, with an explicit accounting of measurement uncertainty.

Lab 2 — Drone Setup

Students assemble an airframe, install the propulsion and avionics stack, configure the flight controller, calibrate sensors, bind and configure the RC link, and complete a pre-flight and functional check-off before the vehicle is cleared to fly.

Lab 3 — Flight Controller

Students implement, tune, and evaluate attitude and altitude control loops on their vehicle, progressing from bench and tethered testing to stabilized flight.

Lab 4 — Motion Capture / Navigation

Using ground-truth pose from the motion capture system, students close position and trajectory-tracking loops, execute generated trajectories, and quantify tracking performance against the models developed earlier in the term.
